The SEBI Mutual Fund Riskometer

Learn how to read the six-tiered visual risk gauge mandated on all scheme documents and marketing materials.

Objective Evaluation

How the Riskometer is Calculated

SEBI introduced a dynamic, formula-driven Riskometer that evaluates actual portfolio holdings on the last day of each calendar month, rather than relying on subjective marketing descriptions.

For debt schemes, the calculation assigns objective mathematical scores across three parameters: Credit Risk (issuer rating), Interest Rate Risk (Macaulay duration), and Liquidity Risk (trading depth).

For equity schemes, scores are derived from Market Capitalization (large, mid, small), Volatility, and Impact Cost (ease of selling underlying shares without price disruption).

The 6 Tiers

Riskometer Classification Spectrum

1. Low Risk

Overnight funds, ultra-short sovereign paper. Minimal capital volatility and maximum credit safety.

2. Low to Moderate Risk

Liquid funds and money market schemes holding top-tier bank CDs and treasury bills.

3. Moderate Risk

Short duration debt funds, corporate bond schemes, and conservative hybrid funds.

4. Moderately High Risk

Balanced advantage funds, dynamic asset allocation schemes, and long-term gilt portfolios.

5. High Risk

Diversified large-cap equity funds, flexi-cap schemes, and aggressive hybrid funds.

6. Very High Risk

Mid-cap, small-cap, sectoral/thematic, and international equity mutual funds.
